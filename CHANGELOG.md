## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The cube yaw (step 3.3c, ADR-012): `geometry.cube_yaw` back-projects the detected contour onto the top-face plane (z = 5 cm) and takes the angle of `cv2.minAreaRect`, in [−45°, 45°) (90° symmetry); `/object_position` carries it as a quaternion about z, where it was the identity, read by the brain as a yaw of 0. On the 32 whole cubes of the reference images: median 0.46°, p90 2.62° (MES-14 expected 1.3° / 8.3°).
- Mutation testing of the arm-protecting modules (step 5.1c, MES-42): mutmut configuration in the `setup.cfg` of `so101_driver` and `so101_brain`, run by hand (`08` §1). `command.py` 92.3 % of the mutants killed, `fsm.py` 92.5 %; the tests it asked for: the rejection reason, an unknown joint name before a known one, the reason of every FSM transition, the lock window after an outlier, the newest detection's freshness, box detections without the cube, the exact staleness age. The surviving mutants are equivalent or exact-threshold ties, listed in MES-42.
- Evaluation bench (step 4.5a, `08` §3): `ros2 run so101_bringup evaluate --episodes N --out <csv> [--bags <dir>] [--seed S] [--timeout 60]`, the CLI the CI and nightly E2E jobs already call. Each episode randomizes, starts from `IDLE`, waits for `DONE`, `ERROR` or the bench timeout, and scores with the ground truth (cube in the box footprint, z < 6 cm). One CSV row per episode, the summary (success rate, p50/p95 of the successful cycles, causes), a rosbag per failed episode; outputs ignored by Git.
- Perception publishes (step 3.3b): `/object_position` (cube center) and `/drop_box_position` (box floor center) as `PoseStamped` in `base_link` with the image's stamp, only for a whole, valid blob; K from `camera_info`, the pose from `extrinsics_file`; a median of 5 per object, a jump over 2 cm rejected unless it persists 5 times (`filter.py`); the HSV thresholds and guards as ROS parameters with ranges, read at each image; the debug image paints the masks (cube green, box blue), the centroids and `OK`/`TRUNCATED`/`LOST`. A launch test replays reference images: poses within 5 mm of the labels, nothing for a truncated cube.
- Brain planning (step 4.2b): `PLAN` runs the grasp planner in a worker process polled by the tick (a worker thread delayed the tick through the GIL: p99 29.4 → 24.4 ms during `PLAN`, MES-40), `/brain/markers` (pre-grasp, grasp, drop, approach arrow), `APPROACH` waits for the gripper open within 3 %, the planner's `grasp.*` parameters in `brain.yaml`; `PLAN` timeout 6 s (a cube out of reach takes 1.7 s to fail, 2.4 s on the CI runner).
- Ground-truth perception stub (step 4.2c, development only): `so101_bringup/test/gt_perception_stub.py` and `gt_stub.launch.py`, outside every pipeline launch file (C-06). A system test runs a full cycle in sim to `DONE`, the cube in the box; 20/20 over 20 randomized scenes, cycle median 14.6 s (MES-40).
- RViz configuration for the demo (step 1.5b): `base_link` fixed frame, the arm model, TF, the camera and perception debug images (best effort), the TCP, cube and box poses, the drop-box marker and the brain's markers; displays of topics still to come are already in the file.
- Perception skeleton (step 3.1): `so101_perception` builds (no more `COLCON_IGNORE`) and runs in the perception container, which builds up to it into `build_perception/`. Each camera image (subscribed Best effort, depth 1) comes back once on `/perception/debug_image` with a "NO DETECTION" status; `perception.launch.py`, `perception.yaml`; the CI perception job runs. A `launch_testing` test feeds it a fake 10 Hz camera.
- Pixel → 3D (step 3.3a, ADR-012): pure `so101_perception/geometry.py`, the MuJoCo → OpenCV camera convention (`FLIP`, `from_mujoco`), `pixel_to_world` on a horizontal plane (`None` when the ray misses it) and `world_to_pixel`, the extrinsics file (`T_base_cam`, frames, method, date, reprojection error; frames and rigidity checked on load). Provisional `config/camera_extrinsics.yaml` from the provided camera pose, replaced at step 3.4. On the reference images, exact pose: cube mean 2.4 mm (max 3.9), box floor center mean 3.1 mm (max 11.5); round trip < 1e-9 m.
- HSV detection (step 3.2b, ADR-011): pure `so101_perception/detection.py` on the rgb8 image, red cube on two hue ranges (around H = 0), brown box, 3×3 opening, largest external contour with 15 px and solidity 0.6, `truncated` when it touches the border, centroid by moments, masks for the debug image; thresholds in a `Thresholds` object (ROS parameters at 3.3b). On the reference images: 32/32 whole cubes, 8/8 truncated flagged, 0 false positive, 44/44 boxes, 0.6 ms per frame.
- Reference images for the perception tests (step 3.2a): 44 seeded sim renders in `so101_perception/test/data/` (32 whole cubes, 8 truncated, 4 out of view, arm at `PARK`, 2.2 MB) with `labels.json` (cube center and yaw, box floor center, view, visible fractions, K, camera pose). The generator `ros2 run so101_bringup make_perception_dataset` is in `so101_bringup`; its test regenerates the committed set.
- Brain state machine (stage 4): pure `fsm.py` with the states of `06` §3 (ADR-015), `step(now, inputs)` on an injected steady clock, target lock, retries, timeouts and error causes, one test per transition and `/brain/stop` from every state (4.1); `brain_node` runs it in a 50 Hz tick, with `/brain/start` and `/brain/stop` answering at once, `/brain_state` latched (transient_local) on each transition and at 1 Hz, `ERROR:no_joint_states` when `/joint_states` stops for 0.5 s, `/go_to_target` in `IDLE` only, and the `grasp.*`/`fsm.*` parameters in `brain.yaml` (4.2a). `fsm.park_pose` moves to elbow 1.5 rad: at 1.6 the lower arm pressed on the shoulder and stayed 0.01 rad short of the reach tolerance, so the cycle never left `WAIT_TARGET` in sim [MES-39].
- Grasp chain (stage 2): orientation IK `ik_point` (approach axis, optional fixed roll) and `ik_pose` in `kinematics.py`, every solution checked by FK (2.1b); pure `grasp_planner.py` (ADR-009): tilt sweep, jaws aligned to a face (the closest of 4) or free roll, pre-grasp 4 cm back, lift and drop keeping the roll, drop with the gripper as vertical as reachable, failure reasons for the FSM (2.4a); closed-loop test in sim, 40/40 cubes in the box with cube and box randomized (2.4b) [MES-38].
- IK accuracy measured in sim (step 2.3, C-41): `so101_bringup/test/test_ik_accuracy.py`, 20 seeded targets through `/go_to_target`, true TCP from MuJoCo's FK of the measured joints, cube and box placed by a seeded `/sim/randomize` and avoided; mean 0.17 mm, max 0.25 mm (`results/ik_accuracy_sim_2026-10-18.md`); gripper within 3 % at 0/50/100 %.
- Driver sim tools (step 1.4): `/sim/randomize` moves the cube (provided `randomize_object()`) and the box, drawn by a pure `randomize.py` in a reachable area fully in the camera view, 0.15 m from the cube; `seed` parameter (same seed, same sequence); `expose_ground_truth` (false by default) publishes `/sim/ground_truth/object` and `drop_box` at 10 Hz for the bench. Launch tests for the joint-limit clamp, NaN and size-mismatch commands (1.5a).
- Driver, rest of stage 1: `/joint_command` clamped to the joint limits and rejected whole on a size mismatch or a NaN/inf, in a pure `command.py` with property tests (1.1a); rates, speed limits and image size as read-only ROS parameters with ranges, values in `so101_bringup/config/driver.yaml` (1.1b); `/external_cam/camera_info` with the image's header, `frame_id` = `external_cam_optical_frame` (1.3a); normalized effort in `/joint_states`, gripper positive when squeezing (1.3b, MES-35).
- `perception` service in `docker-compose.yml` (C-02), on the same DDS domain and transport as `control`; idle until step 3.1. `docker compose up -d control perception viz` now matches the brief, and the E2E and nightly jobs can start the stack.
- Brain motion (stage 2): FK/IK in a pure `kinematics.py` (ikpy behind a façade, seeds clipped into the bounds, fallback seeds for targets past the pan range, MES-36), `/end_effector_pose` at 30 Hz, min-jerk `trajectory.py`, and `/go_to_target` answering at once then streaming the trajectory at 50 Hz (`motion.*` parameters in `brain.yaml`); FK checked against MuJoCo (0.0033 mm).
- `so101_bringup` (step 0.3): `sim.launch.py` starts the driver and the brain (`use_sim` and `port` forwarded), parameter files in `config/`, and the RViz file moved to `rviz/so101.rviz`; the control container starts it. A `launch_testing` test checks both nodes and the forwarded arguments.
- Sim camera (step 1.2): `/external_cam/image_raw` (`rgb8`, 320×240, 10 Hz) rendered in a dedicated thread on a copy of the sim state, behind a backend lock; OSMesa render threads limited to half the cores so the control period keeps p99 20.4 ms (MES-34). `launch_testing` checks the image and a clean SIGINT stop.
- Test tooling (0.3d): Hypothesis property tests, coverage with a comment on each PR (python-coverage-comment-action, non-blocking), and colcon-ros-domain-id-coordinator so `colcon test` never shares a DDS domain with a running stack or a parallel run; one 11.5 MB layer in both images.
- Base driver (step 1.0): `/joint_states` at 30 Hz (gripper in rad, stamped at read time), `/joint_command` with partial and unnamed commands, a speed limiter (1.5 rad/s, gripper 200 %/s) in a pure `limiter.py`; unit and `launch_testing` tests. The real arm stays limp until 6.1a.
- `so101_common`: joint names, joint limits, gripper %↔rad conversion and frame names defined once, tested against the URDF (ADR-003); the driver and brain stubs drop their local copies.
- Repository architecture: CI (lint, guards, build, tests), nightly, school mirror, governance files, project documentation.
- Planning conventions: GitHub Project (epics, PR-sized tickets, fields, automations), a single ticket-anatomy issue template, `Closes #` in the PR template; `AGENTS.md` (+ `CLAUDE.md` import) for AI coding agents.
- Phase 2: Connect Four against the robot, validated by the teaching staff (`docs/project/phase2/`, ADR-034 to 038), with its own roadmap (`phase2/P2-04`) and its kinematics and camera measurements (MES-27 to 32); store-bought grid on a riser, depth guide, token handover, wrist camera (ADR-038); real-arm calibration-zero check (MES-30, `07` §4).

### Fixed

- Sim `camera_info`: the principal point is now in OpenCV pixel coordinates, (159.5, 119.5) at 320×240 instead of the provided (160, 120), which put pixel edges on integers. Every OpenCV centroid was half a pixel off: cube error 2.63 → 0.50 mm mean over 50 scenes (MES-41, #122), 2.39 → 0.78 mm on the reference images. The reference images' `labels.json` carries the same K; the images are unchanged.
- Driver and brain: timers and motion time on the steady clock. On WSL2 the system clock steps back ~2.6 s every 30 s: `/joint_states` stopped and motions froze for 2.6 s at each step; largest `/joint_states` gap now 37 ms [MES-37].
- Control container shutdown: SIGINT goes to `ros2 launch` only, which forwards one to each node; signalling the whole process group gave each node a second SIGINT that interrupted `destroy_node()` (traceback on every `docker compose stop`).
- Driver: SIGINT or SIGTERM while a timer callback publishes no longer ends in a traceback and exit code 1 (seen once in 40 SIGTERM stops).
