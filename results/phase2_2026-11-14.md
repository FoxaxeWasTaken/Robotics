# Phase 2 in simulation: Connect Four with the dispenser and the positions from perception (#259, #260), 2026-11-14

The game as it ships: the robot takes its tokens at the dispenser (`token_source: dispenser`), and where the grid and the dispenser stand comes from the camera only. Measured on the development machine, in throwaway containers, each stack alone on its ROS domain, on the code of #260. The nightly of 2026-11-13 ([`phase2_2026-11-13.md`](phase2_2026-11-13.md)) ran before the dispenser, the robot's tokens given by hand; the next nightly on `main` replaces the games' figures below. Ground truth scores only (C-06).

To run it again: `tools/so101 bench --game -n 10`; the 50 picks and drops: `CONNECT4_DROPS=50` on `so101_bringup/test/test_connect4_drop_launch.py`; a fault case: `tools/so101 robustness g1` to `g8`.

## The ten games

Per game: `phase2_2026-11-14_games.csv` (the columns played in order, the seconds, the cause of a failure).

- 99 tokens, 7 to 16 a game; the robot won the ten games. None lost, none in another column.
- A robot's turn: its search 0.2 to 0.6 s, its move **20.5 to 22.4 s** (the mean of each game): a pick then the drop. With the token given by hand it was 11.7 to 12.7 s.
- A game lasts 86.5 to 190.1 s, against 51 to 112 s with the token given by hand.

## Where the grid can stand

The brain judges each move on the measured pose before the arm moves (`docs/project/phase2/P2-02` §2.5, ADR-042).

| Grid | Nearest the arm passes, in truth | The game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where the layout puts it | 11.2 mm (upper arm, column 6) | Played |
| Carried ±10° around the arm, or ±5 mm across | 11.1 to 11.2 mm | Played |
| 10 mm nearer the arm | 8.1 mm | Played |
| 20 mm nearer | 2.1 mm (shoulder, near post) | Refused, `unsafe_path` |
| 5 mm further | 3.5 mm (lower arm, guide's end) | Refused, `unsafe_path` |
| Turned on itself by 1.5° or more towards the fixed jaw | A token falls beside its column | Refused, `not_edge_on` |

- The brain's model of the arm says what MuJoCo says within 1.4 mm on 99 % of 68,524 poses, 3.6 mm at most; it refuses a move under 5 mm [MES-78].
- A drop works from −8.8° to +1.2° off the arm's plane, a pick from −4.4° to +5.7° without touching the dispenser [MES-79].

## What the other measurements say

- The grid read by the camera: 2100/2100 cells over 50 random states, under the default light and a dim one [MES-68, MES-73].
- The dispenser placed by the camera: its stations within 1.4 mm over 20 placements [MES-76].
- Mutation testing of what decides when and how the arm moves: `turns.py` 411 of 431 mutants caught, `drop.py` 508 of 570, `clearance.py` 127 of 136, `placement.py` 123 of 137 [MES-77, MES-78].

## Limits

- Simulation only: the real arm is `v2.1.0` (`docs/project/phase2/README.md`).
- One machine, not the CI's runner: the game's nightly figures are those of 2026-11-13 until a nightly runs on this code.
- On a machine also running the test suites and a mutation run, the brain's tick slowed to 40 to 75 ms and one pick of one fault case ended `no_joint_states`; alone, the stack keeps 20.6 ms and the case is handled.
- The scripted person is random, and seed 0 replays the same ten games.
