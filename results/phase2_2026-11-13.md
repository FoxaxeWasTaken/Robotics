## Limits

- Simulation only: the real arm is `v2.1.0` (`docs/project/phase2/README.md`).
- The scripted person is random: the robot wins in 7 to 16 tokens. Against the engine at its medium level a game lasts up to 40 tokens (2 of 2, MES-71); the nightly does not play those.
- One seed (0) each night: the ten games are the same from night to night until the code changes what the robot plays.
